Wild Rice

Odin Recipes

A healthy wild rice dish with lots of veggies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup wild rice
  • 4 diced carrots
  • 4 thinly sliced mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup of dried cranberries
  • 1 bag of frozen peas
  • 1 bunch of thinly sliced green onions
  • 1 tablespoon of soy sauce

Steps

  1. Add 2 cups of water to a medium sized pot and bring it to a boil
  2. Wash and add the wild rice to the boiling water, and set a timer for 25 minutes
  3. In a large pot, saute the carrots, and mushrooms
  4. Once the wild rice is done cooking, add it to the large pot
  5. Add the peas and thinly sliced green onions to the large pot
  6. Add the soy sauce and stir it all together
  7. Enjoy!
