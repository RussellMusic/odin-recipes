Egg Frittata

Odin Recipes

A hearty egg frittata

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 diced carrots
  • 2 Bell peppers
  • 4 thinly sliced mushrooms
  • 1/2 bag of frozen peas
  • 1 bunch of thinly sliced green onions
  • 4 ounces of shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Pre-heat the oven to 350F
  2. Saute the carrots, bell peppers, and mushrooms in a large cast iron skillet
  3. Beat the eggs with a wisk and add the salt
  4. Add the eggs to the skillet
  5. Add the green onions and peas to the skillet and stir them in
  6. Add the shredded cheese on top
  7. Cook in the oven for at least 10 minutes or until the center is no longer runny
  8. Turn on the broiler for 2 minutes
  9. Enjoy!
