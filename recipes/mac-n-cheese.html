A quick and easy mac n cheese

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of elbow macoroni
  • 12 ounces of shredded cheese
  • 12 ounces of evaporated milk
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Add the pasta to a large pot of water with just enough water to cover it
  2. Bring the water to a boil
  3. Cook for 10 minutes
  4. Drain and save the pasta water
  5. Add in the evaporated milk
  6. Once the evaporated milk starts to boil, cut the head and stir in the shredded cheese
  7. Gradually add in the pasta water until you reach your desired consistency
  8. Add 1 teaspoon of salt
  9. Enjoy!
